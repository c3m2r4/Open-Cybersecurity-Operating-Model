## Governance Principle

Cybersecurity governance exists to make sure security decisions are tied to business objectives, risk appetite, accountability, and evidence. It should not become documentation theater.

## Business-to-Risk Chain

```text
Business Objective
  -> Business Process
  -> Critical Asset
  -> Threat Scenario
  -> Risk Event
  -> Control Objective
  -> Control Owner
  -> Evidence
  -> Residual Risk
  -> Management Decision
```
