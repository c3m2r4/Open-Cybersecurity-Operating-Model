# Control - Access Review

## Control Objective

Detect and correct inappropriate, stale, excessive, or unauthorized access.

## Risk Addressed

Excessive access, dormant accounts, leaver access, privilege creep, segregation-of-duties conflicts.

## Control Type

Detective and corrective. Administrative.

## Control Owner

VERIFY.

## Implementation Guidance

Define review population, reviewer accountability, review frequency, evidence, remediation workflow, escalation, and closure validation. Frequency is organization-specific: VERIFY.

## Evidence

Access review campaign records, reviewer decisions, entitlement exports, removal tickets, exception approvals, escalation records.

## Control Testing

Confirm the complete population was reviewed, reviewers were appropriate, decisions were recorded, and removals were completed.

## Failure Conditions

Incomplete population, rubber-stamp approvals, no remediation evidence, missing privileged access, no owner, overdue campaigns.

## Metrics

Review completion, removal completion, overdue reviews, reviewer exception rate, dormant accounts.
