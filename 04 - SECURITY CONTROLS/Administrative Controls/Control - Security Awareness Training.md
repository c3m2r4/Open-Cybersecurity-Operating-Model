# Control - Security Awareness Training

## Control Objective

Improve security behaviors and reduce human-centered risk through practical, role-aware education.

## Risk Addressed

Phishing, social engineering, data mishandling, weak reporting, insecure development behavior.

## Control Type

Preventive and deterrent. Administrative.

## Control Owner

VERIFY.

## Implementation Guidance

Use baseline awareness, phishing reporting education, role-based training, privileged-user training, developer training, and incident reporting reinforcement.

## Evidence

Training assignments, completion reports, phishing simulation records, reported phishing metrics, role-based training records.

## Control Testing

Assess assignment coverage, completion, role relevance, reporting behavior, and follow-up for repeated high-risk behavior.

## Failure Conditions

Annual completion only, no role-based training, no reporting path, repeated risky behavior without follow-up.

## Metrics

Phishing report rate, repeat click rate, role-based coverage, training completion, incident reporting volume.
