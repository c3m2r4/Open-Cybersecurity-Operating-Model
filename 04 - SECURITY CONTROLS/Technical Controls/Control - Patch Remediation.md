# Control - Patch Remediation

## Control Objective

Correct known vulnerabilities through patching, upgrade, configuration change, mitigation, or compensating controls.

## Risk Addressed

Exploitation of known vulnerabilities, malware infection, privilege escalation, service compromise.

## Control Type

Corrective. Administrative and technical.

## Control Owner

VERIFY.

## Implementation Guidance

Prioritize by asset criticality, exploitability, exposure, compensating controls, and business impact. CVSS alone is not business risk.

## Evidence

Vulnerability records, patch deployment reports, change tickets, exception approvals, retest results, compensating control evidence.

## Control Testing

Validate remediation through scan, configuration check, version check, or manual verification.

## Failure Conditions

Overdue critical vulnerabilities, unsupported systems, failed deployments, no retest, permanent exceptions.

## Metrics

Critical vulnerabilities beyond SLA, mean remediation time, retest pass rate, accepted exceptions.
