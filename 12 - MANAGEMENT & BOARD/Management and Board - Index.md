# Management and Board - Index

Use this area to translate cybersecurity evidence into business decisions.

## Executive Questions

| Question | Source Evidence |
|---|---|
| What risk exists? | Risk assessment, finding, incident report |
| Why does it matter? | Business context, asset criticality, impact analysis |
| How likely is it? | Threat activity, exploitability, exposure, control effectiveness |
| What is the impact? | Financial, operational, legal, safety, reputational impact |
| What choices exist? | Mitigate, accept, transfer, avoid |
| What investment is required? | People, process, technology, time |
| What decision is needed? | Approval, funding, exception, risk acceptance |
