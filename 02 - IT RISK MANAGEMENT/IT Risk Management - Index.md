# IT Risk Management - Index

Use this area to identify, assess, analyze, treat, accept, monitor, and report technology risk.

## Risk Workflow

1. Identify the asset and business process.
2. Define the risk scenario.
3. Assess likelihood and impact.
4. Identify existing controls.
5. Test control design and operation.
6. Rate residual risk.
7. Define treatment or acceptance.
8. Report the decision required.

## Professional Boundary

Risk management notes in this vault describe method and example structures. They do not establish actual organizational risk ratings, risk appetite, control effectiveness, or acceptance authority unless supported by evidence.

Use:

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `VERIFY` | Confirm in the target environment |
| `NOT YET ESTABLISHED` | Required governance element is missing from the vault |
| `EXAMPLE` | Teaching example, not a factual claim |
