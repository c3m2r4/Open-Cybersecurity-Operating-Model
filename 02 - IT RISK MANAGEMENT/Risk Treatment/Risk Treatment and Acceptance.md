# Risk Treatment and Acceptance

## Overview

Risk treatment is the accountable decision about what to do with residual risk.

## Treatment Options

| Option | Use When | Evidence Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Mitigate | Risk should be reduced through control improvement | Treatment plan, owner, target date, expected risk reduction |
| Accept | Residual risk is within tolerance or temporary exception is justified | Risk owner approval, expiry, compensating controls, review date |
| Transfer | Financial or contractual impact can be partially shifted | Contract, insurance, third-party terms, retained risk analysis |
| Avoid | The activity creating risk should stop | Business decision, decommission plan, migration plan |

## Acceptance Requirements

Risk acceptance should include:

| Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|
| Risk scenario | Clear statement of what could happen |
| Business impact | Consequence management is accepting |
| Residual risk | What remains after controls |
| Compensating controls | Temporary or alternative safeguards |
| Expiration date | Prevents indefinite exception |
| Acceptance authority | Person or forum authorized to accept |
| Review trigger | Date, project milestone, incident, regulatory change, threat change |

## Professional Judgment

Risk acceptance is not a technical sign-off by the security team. Security can advise and validate evidence, but the accountable business or risk owner should accept business risk.
